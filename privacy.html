Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Discord Profile Manager (the “App”), an independent Windows desktop utility operated by NitroSquad (the “Operator”), handles information. Effective November 2, 2026.

1. What information the App processes

When you create or edit presets, the App stores information you enter, such as preset names, usernames and display names, bios, pronouns, statuses, profile colors, server labels, selected cosmetic references, and artwork files you import. It also stores your self-reported list of cosmetics you own or can use, including optional cosmetic SKU IDs and preview images.

If you choose Check live profile, the App asks Discord for the identify OAuth2 permission through your browser. Discord may return your account ID, username, global display name, current avatar and banner identifiers, accent color, equipped avatar decoration and nameplate metadata, including available SKU IDs. The App uses that information to display a current-profile snapshot and compare it with your saved presets. It may download the associated avatar, banner, and decoration preview images from Discord's content delivery network.

The App saves the public Discord OAuth application Client ID you enter, for convenience. It does not ask for your Discord password or private account token, and its OAuth access/refresh tokens are not written to disk.

2. How information is used

  • To save and display your profile designs and manually maintained cosmetic library.
  • To preview designs and guide you through an assisted, manual profile swap.
  • To show your latest voluntarily requested current profile check and compare it with presets.
  • To create local backups when you choose to export or restore them.

The App does not automatically apply profile changes to your Discord account or independently verify whether a cosmetic was purchased.

3. Where data is stored and when it is sent

The desktop App stores preset data, snapshots and imported/downloaded images on your computer in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DiscordProfileManager\, including library.json and the assets folder. Backup ZIP files are written to the location you select. The Operator does not run a separate App account database or receive your local preset library through the described functionality.

For optional sign-in and profile checking, the App communicates directly from your computer with Discord's authorization and API services. To retrieve selected artwork, it communicates with cdn.discordapp.com. Discord independently processes requests under its own Privacy Policy. Opening these legal pages also connects your browser to the website's hosting provider, which may maintain standard web access logs. The static pages do not include an App-operated analytics or advertising script.

The App does not request your messages, direct messages, server list, purchase history or email address and does not transmit your profile library to a cloud sync service. Your own decision to share a backup file is outside the Operator's control.

4. Data retention and deletion

Saved presets, self-reported cosmetic records and profile snapshots remain on your computer until you remove them. In the App, Clear saved snapshot clears the stored snapshot record, but may leave downloaded images or data included in existing presets or backups. Deleting a preset or cosmetic entry may likewise leave separately stored artwork files.

To erase all locally stored App data, close the App, then delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\DiscordProfileManager\ and any backup files you exported elsewhere. Uninstalling or deleting the program itself may not remove these separately stored files. This deletion does not remove information retained by Discord, your backup destinations, or your website host.

To revoke Discord access, open Discord → User Settings → Authorized Apps and remove the developer application you previously authorized. Revoking access and deleting local data are separate steps.

For a privacy inquiry or deletion assistance, contact the Operator using the email below. Because the App described here does not operate an Operator-hosted user database, the Operator generally cannot directly erase files kept solely on your device.

5. Sharing and security

The described App does not sell profile information or send it to advertising networks. Locally exported backups contain your saved profile data and images: treat them as personal files, and share only with people you trust. Access to local storage is governed by the security of your Windows account and computer. No security measure can be guaranteed to eliminate every risk.

6. Changes and eligibility

This policy may be updated if the App's features or data practices change. The current version should be available at this page, with its effective date updated. The App is intended only for people who are eligible to use Discord in their jurisdiction.
